# Terms Draft

Papertrade Online is educational paper-trading software.

It does not provide investment advice, research advice, or a recommendation to buy or sell securities.

The application is intended to use read-only market data and browser-local simulated trades. It must not place, modify, or cancel real broker orders.

Users are responsible for:

- Their own broker account
- Their own Groww API access or subscription costs
- Their own credentials
- Any trading decisions taken outside this application

Market data may be delayed, stale, incorrect, or unavailable.

The software is provided as-is, without warranties.
